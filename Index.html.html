How progression works

Log working sets only, no warm-ups. Enter the load and the reps you actually got.

Stay at the same load until every set reaches the top of the rep range, then add the increment shown and start again at the bottom. Load only moves when all sets get there, including the last one.

Adding reps at the same load is progress. A week that goes from 5,5,5 to 6,6,5 counts, even though the number on the bar did not move.

Judge progress over three weeks, not one session. Performance fluctuates and a single flat week is noise. Three weeks with no added load or reps anywhere on an exercise is a stall: drop about 10 per cent and rebuild.

Reps left in tank is how many more you could have done on your hardest set. Aim for 1 to 3 on most work and 2 to 3 on the heavy compounds. Tap the same number again to clear it.

Rest two to three minutes on the first two movements and about 90 seconds on the rest. Under a minute measurably costs you. If time is short, superset two unrelated accessories rather than cutting rest on the compounds.
