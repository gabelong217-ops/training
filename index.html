How progression works

Log working sets only, no warm-ups. Enter the load and the reps you actually got.

Stay at the same load until every set reaches the top of the rep range, then add the increment shown and start again at the bottom. Load only moves when all sets get there, including the last one.

Reps left in tank is how many more you could have done on your hardest set. Aim for 1 to 3 on most work and 2 to 3 on the heavy compounds. It is optional, but without it a plateau in the numbers cannot be told apart from sets quietly getting easier. Tap the same number again to clear it.

Rest two to three minutes on the first two movements and about 90 seconds on the rest. Under a minute measurably costs you. If time is short, superset two unrelated accessories rather than cutting rest on the compounds.

Two or three sessions with no added reps anywhere is a real stall. Drop about 10 per cent and rebuild.

Finish session files the day into your history and clears the sheet for next time. Until you press it, your entries stay put as a draft.

Your log lives in this browser only. It survives closing the tab and restarting the phone, but not clearing your browsing data or switching to another device. Press Back up log every few weeks and keep the text in a note.
